CRITICAL PROTOCOL REQUIREMENT (DUAL-PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE):

The original DARWIN models (Extra Trees, Random Forest, SVM) were trained on digitizing tablets capturing physical pen tip force and in-air electromagnetic hover. Finger touchscreen handwriting lacks calibrated pen pressure and continuous in-air trajectories. Feeding uncalibrated finger-touch data into the original 450-feature model without explicit domain adaptation is scientifically invalid.
